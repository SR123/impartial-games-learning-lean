SEMANTIC FINDINGS
1. RealNoise uses genuine Mathlib real arithmetic. Its adapters verify that the
   generic field operations are the intended operations. mUp = ceil(4/a^2) is a
   natural sample count, ach_exists establishes existence, and mMin_spec proves
   attainment/minimality. For 0<a<=1/2 and worst-case error <=1/4,
        3/(32a^2) <= mMin <= ceil(4/a^2) <= 17/(4a^2).
   The lower bound covers randomized decision rules, not just majority.
   Finite nonnegative product probabilities, marginals, fresh-block observations
   and real flip parameters are connected. Zero samples, no signal and exact
   signal are distinguished. Reusing the same corrupted bit gives no new data.
   The numerical 31-repetition result is sufficient, not a claim of optimality.
   The manuscript now defines the minimum explicitly and gives a short proof.

2. Fourier/RealFourier establish orthonormality, inversion, uniqueness, Parseval,
   real finite-cube sums, Fourier tails, band limitation and the correlation
   implication. Nim-bit degree N and known-heap delta degree 2 are connected to
   actual bit encodings. The total OR-over-heaps of XORs agrees with the delta
   on at-most-one-change pairs. For N>=2 its sign is not a signed character;
   N=1 is explicitly degree two. This is now the exact claim in the paper.
   Claude also proves uniqueness of the degree-2N character agreeing on ALL
   single-change pairs, including increases and unchanged boards. That theorem
   must not be advertised as a legal-decreases-only result. The paper does not
   make that stronger assertion.

3. WeakMastery formalises roles, histories, deterministic strategies, legal
   opponents, forfeits, terminating play and winning guarantees. The equivalence
   to moving to a P-position on all policy-consistent decision histories is
   substantive. Initially losing roles give vacuous weak mastery. Pairing has
   a compiled selector and a genuine second-player guarantee; multi-head and
   two-frame strategies are instantiated. Strong mastery uses arbitrary legal
   histories, and the (3)->(0) example separates it from weak mastery.
   The manuscript now specifies these domains and gives that simple example.

4. The latest paper contained stale implementation language. BoardStore already
   checks one-pass storage (NB+B+r bits, N+1 updates plus reset) as well as the
   replay alternative. Phase 7 already checks the outer early-exit and all-head
   schedules, including actual rollout execution, reset and head switching.
   The updated paper distinguishes classification from move selection and counts
   the read-only original board separately from controller registers. It records
   M+2B+2 component evaluations for all heads plus final readout, not just M
   simulated legal moves. The circuits and networks linked to these semantics
   have depth <=34 and layers <=70 per controller tick. These are sufficient
   resource bounds, not hardware timings or optimised implementations.

REMAINING BOUNDARIES AND OPTIONAL FORMAL WORK
A. Classical results are intentionally external. HastadNegl is a theorem
   parameter, not a hidden Lean axiom. The main paper-specific application to
   AC0 readouts, finite probabilities and the negligible final error is checked.
   The analytic passage from a published exponential correlation estimate to
   that named negligible-family theorem remains at the accepted classical
   boundary. General Sprague-Grundy, Ajtai and other cited background are also
   literature results, not claimed as newly formalised here.
B. Fourier.TailPremise is not instantiated against one precise LMN/Tal theorem
   with reconciled size/depth conventions. The tail-to-correlation implication
   is checked. Completing that adapter is useful final boundary work, but is
   not needed for the paper's principal search bound, whose correlation route
   is separate. The revised verification paragraph discloses this explicitly.
C. WholeGame.ReplayWins is not instantiated in the newer Mastery.Hist model.
   This is an optional unification bridge: the replay game and network guarantee
   already exist in their original semantics. Do not describe it as an absent
   recurrent winning proof. The same distinction applies to storage semantics.
D. Strategies in the new general game framework are deterministic; probability
   over testing rules is formalised separately. No randomized game-policy or
   learning-convergence theorem is claimed. A universal adaptive-FSM compiler
   is not claimed; the two concrete schedules are checked.
E. Training curves, checkpoint observations, plateau causation and empirical
   implementation are not kernel-certified. This audit did not rerun training.
   Mathematical baseline counts are checked; they do not establish why SGD
   remains near 0.54 or when it improves. The paper already states this carefully.

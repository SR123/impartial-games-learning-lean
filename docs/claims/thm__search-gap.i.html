Theorem 5.12 (i)One structured head plus an AC0 readout wins with probability at most 2/(2^B−1)+negl(N)

2 Explain this

The setting. Boards are uniformly random NNN-heap boards with BBB-bit heaps, conditioned on being winning (σ≠0\sigma\ne0σ=0). The agent observes one structured channel β=ROπj(P)\beta=\mathrm{RO}_{\pi_j}(P)β=ROπj​​(P) and must output a move with a shallow circuit FN(P,β)F_N(P,\beta)FN​(P,β).

The bound. Its chance of playing a winning move is at most 2/(2B−1)2/(2^B-1)2/(2B−1) plus a quantity that vanishes faster than any inverse polynomial in NNN. For B=2B=2B=2 that is about 2/32/32/3; for B=4B=4B=4 about 0.130.130.13.

Intuition. Knowing one linear bit ℓ(σ)\ell(\sigma)ℓ(σ) narrows the 2B−12^B-12B−1 possible nonzero nim-sums to a fibre of about half of them. A shallow readout has essentially no correlation with the remaining parity information, so the best it can do is guess one value per fibre: two lucky values out of 2B−12^B-12B−1.

Attainability. Guessing one nonzero value in each fibre achieves 2/(2B−1)2/(2^B-1)2/(2B−1) exactly, so the constant is right.

Caveats. Fixed BBB; this distribution; this interface. The negligible term rests on Håstad's parity-correlation bound, used as the named external input HastadNegl. It is not a bound on general single-policy search or on MCTS.

3 How this is formalised

Finite part, no classical input. Prob.success_bound: for every readout and every η\etaη, the correlation premise Prob.CorrHyp (∣E[Hb,aχu]∣≤η|\mathbb E[H_{b,a}\chi_u]|\le\eta∣E[Hb,a​χu​]∣≤η for all observed bits bbb, values aaa and nonzero uuu) implies conditional success at most 2/(q−1)+(q−1)η2/(q-1)+(q-1)\eta2/(q−1)+(q−1)η, q=2Bq=2^Bq=2B.

From circuits to the premise. CorrBridge.circuit_readout_bound: for readouts given by formulas of depth ddd and size sss, the premise follows from the parity-correlation premise for formulas of depth d+11d+11d+11 and polynomially larger size on every support size between N+1N+1N+1 and B(N+1)B(N+1)B(N+1), where Lean indexes boards by N+1N+1N+1 heaps (so this is NNN to BNBNBN in the paper's notation); restriction and averaging are checked.

Asymptotic part, named classical input. ClassicalBoundary.search_gap_asymptotic: assuming ClassicalBoundary.HastadNegl, for fixed B≥2B\ge2B≥2, j<Bj<Bj<B and readout families of fixed depth and polynomial size there is a negligible η\etaη (ClassicalBoundary.Negligible) with success at most 2/(2B−1)+η(N)2/(2^B-1)+\eta(N)2/(2B−1)+η(N). ClassicalBoundary.search_gap_asymptotic_net is the same for fixed-scale network readouts.

Attainability. Attain.attain_exact: for B≥2B\ge2B≥2 and any nonzero functional, the least-representative readout succeeds with conditional probability exactly 2/(2B−1)2/(2^B-1)2/(2B−1), for every N≥1N\ge1N≥1.

Boundary. The analytic derivation of Håstad's correlation bound is external. Everything that applies it to this paper (indicators, restrictions, conditioning, the probability calculation) is checked.

The finite bound holds under the explicit correlation premise CorrHyp (proved without any classical input). The asymptotic form uses the named input HastadNegl, from which the premise is derived for the readout indicators; the analytic derivation of Håstad's bound is external. Attainability of 2/(2B−1)2/(2^B-1)2/(2B−1) is proved exactly.

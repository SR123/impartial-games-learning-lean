2 Explain this

Parity is a pure character. The parity of the inputs in a nonempty set SSS is exactly χS\chi_SχS​, so its Fourier expansion has one coefficient, 111 at SSS. Its frequency is ∣S∣|S|∣S∣.

The inequality. The correlation of fff with χS\chi_SχS​ is the coefficient f^(S)\hat f(S)f^​(S). If ∣S∣>k|S|>k∣S∣>k, its square is one term of the tail above kkk, hence at most ε\varepsilonε for a band-limited fff. So: little high-frequency mass implies little correlation with high-degree parities.

This elementary step is fully proved; what is external is the claim that AC0 functions are band-limited.

2 Explain this

The full proof of Theorem 5.12 has three parts.

(i) With uniform independent heaps, the nim-sum is exactly uniform on F2B\mathbb F_2^BF2B​. Fix the observed bit bbb: the readout becomes a fixed shallow circuit proposing a move, which determines a proposed nim-sum gb(P)g_b(P)gb​(P); the move wins exactly when gβ(P)=σ(P)g_\beta(P)=\sigma(P)gβ​(P)=σ(P). Expand the event σ=a\sigma=aσ=a into its 2B2^B2B parity characters; each nonconstant character is a parity on at least NNN input bits, so a shallow indicator has tiny correlation with it (the classical input). Summing the disjoint events and conditioning on σ≠0\sigma\ne0σ=0 gives 2/(q−1)+(q−1)ηN2/(q-1)+(q-1)\eta_N2/(q−1)+(q−1)ηN​. Fixing all coordinates outside a character's support keeps the circuit shallow, which is how the classical bound for full parity applies. Guessing one nonzero value per fibre attains the leading constant; for B=2B=2B=2 and π0\pi_0π0​ the guesses a0=2a_0=2a0​=2, a1=1a_1=1a1​=1 win with probability exactly 2/32/32/3.

(ii) and (iii) are the multi-head and two-frame constructions.

3 How this is formalised

  • Prob.success_bound: the finite calculation, including uniformity of σ\sigmaσ, character orthogonality, the exact-value expansion, disjointness and conditioning.
  • CorrBridge.circuit_readout_bound: the circuit indicators, restriction to a character's support and averaging over fixed coordinates, from the parity-correlation premise.
  • ClassicalBoundary.search_gap_asymptotic: the asymptotic conclusion from the named input HastadNegl.
  • Attain.attain_exact and Attain.paper_example_two_thirds: attainability, and the B=2B=2B=2 example with success exactly 2/32/32/3 for every N≥1N\ge1N≥1.

Parts (ii) and (iii) are Theorem 5.12 (ii) and Theorem 5.12 (iii).

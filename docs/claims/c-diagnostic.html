Section 7.2.5, paragraph 2Checkpoint diagnostic: almost all remaining errors differ from the required change only in the lowest bit

Empirical result Supported by saved experimental runs, not by a proof.

1 Paper text

A diagnostic of the saved checkpoints makes the remaining difficulty more concrete. For a predicted move setting heap iii to vvv, compare its proposed change pi′⊕vp'_i\oplus vpi′​⊕v with the required nim-sum of p′p'p′. On the reconstructed and independently verified 100,000-position test set, the epoch-23 model makes only legal moves and gets the upper three bits of this change jointly correct on 99.998% of examples. Of its 46,206 failed moves, 46,204 differ from the correct change only in the lowest bit. At epoch 45 all 12,437 errors are confined to that bit; at epoch 178 only two errors remain, both of the same kind. These diagnostic counts weight examples equally, unlike the saved batch-mean accuracies plotted above.

2 Explain this

A closer look at the saved checkpoints makes the plateau concrete. For a predicted move "set heap iii to vvv", compare the change it makes, pi′⊕vp'_i\oplus vpi′​⊕v, with the change needed (the nim-sum of p′p'p′):

  • at epoch 23 the model makes only legal moves and gets the upper three bits of the change right on 99.998% of the 100,000 test positions; of its 46,206 failures, 46,204 are wrong only in the lowest bit;
  • at epoch 45 all 12,437 errors are in that bit; at epoch 178 only two errors remain, both of that kind.

These counts weight examples equally, unlike the batch-mean accuracies in the figures.

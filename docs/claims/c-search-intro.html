1 Paper text

Theorem 5.2 shows that no AC0\AC^0AC0 circuit can evaluate the nim-sum in a single forward pass. A natural follow-up is whether planning—testing candidate moves and simulating play, as in Monte-Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)—can compensate.

2 Explain this

The single-frame barrier rules out computing the needed invariant in one shallow pass. The natural next question is whether planning (simulating play) can make up for it. This subsection studies a deliberately simple planning interface to answer that question precisely.

3 How this is formalised

A restatement of Theorem 5.2 (barrier), whose precise content is winning-move selection on the encoded family; the paper's phrase "evaluate the nim-sum" is the informal reading of that result.

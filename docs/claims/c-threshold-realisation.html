Appendix C, “Realisation with fixed-scale threshold gates”Compiler from formulas to {0,1}-weight threshold networks: ≤2d+2 layers, ≤(2d+1)S+K gates, widths ≤ S+n

1 Paper text

Realisation with fixed-scale threshold gates. An OR gate uses weights 111 and threshold 111; a negated literal supplies NOT. Implement AND by De Morgan, as an OR of negated inputs followed by negation. This keeps the threshold bound fixed even when fan-in grows. Constants use no inputs and threshold 000 or 111. Thus all these circuits can use the fixed grid W=D=1W=D=1W=D=1. More precisely, for KKK output formulas of depth at most ddd, total formula size SSS, and input support below nnn, the checked compiler constructs one network of at most 2d+22d+22d+2 layers, at most (2d+1)S+K(2d+1)S+K(2d+1)S+K gates, and layer widths and fan-ins at most S+nS+nS+n. Identity buffers align layers; every output is proved equal to its source formula on the same finite input. Size counts duplicated formula nodes, and depth counts NOT gates, so these are conservative bounds.

2 Explain this

Any of these Boolean circuits can be turned into a network of the paper's fixed-scale gates with weights and thresholds in {0,1}\{0,1\}{0,1}: an OR gate is a threshold-1 gate; NOT is a negated literal; AND is built by De Morgan from ORs and negations, so no threshold ever needs to equal a growing fan-in. For KKK output formulas of depth at most ddd and total size SSS on fewer than nnn inputs, the compiler builds one network with at most 2d+22d+22d+2 layers, at most (2d+1)S+K(2d+1)S+K(2d+1)S+K gates, and widths and fan-ins at most S+nS+nS+n, and every output equals its formula on every input.

The width counts every node of every output formula, duplicates included, so it is far from economical. These numbers describe the uniform construction, not an optimised network and not a trained one.

How this relates to the Lean proof. The grid is the output of ThresholdNet.compileF as Lean evaluates it on these formulas; the browser only evaluates the exported gates. ThresholdNet.compileF_spec proves, for any list of formulas of depth at most ddd, that the network has at most 2d+22d+22d+2 layers and computes every formula exactly. The selector bounds in the table of Appendix C come from this rule: Family.rest_ac0 (Boolean depth at most 20) and Family.rest_net (at most 42 layers with weights and thresholds 0 or 1).

What this does not show. The 42-layer figure is an upper bound for one explicit, deliberately uniform construction. It is not a lower bound, not a layer count measured in an experiment, and not a statement about which networks training finds. A more economical network may use fewer layers: for the small boards in the table, the selector formulas Lean builds have depth 15, so even the uniform compiler produces 32 layers, below the bound of 42. Proving a sharper general bound would need a separate construction and proof.

3 How this is formalised

  • ThresholdNet.compileF_spec: for formulas reading inputs below nnn with depth at most DDD, the network ThresholdNet.compileF has exact outputs (and false beyond them), is valid for W=1W=1W=1, reads only the nnn inputs in its first layer, has at most 2D+22D+22D+2 layers, widths and fan-ins at most S+nS+nS+n, and at most (2D+1)S+K(2D+1)S+K(2D+1)S+K gates.
  • ThresholdNet.compileF_fixed_grid: every compiled gate fires in the paper's rational form with D=1D=1D=1 exactly when it fires in integer form.
  • ThresholdNet.compileF_bounded: the width and fan-in bound.

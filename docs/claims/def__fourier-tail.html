2 Explain this

The tail above degree kkk is the total squared Fourier weight on sets larger than kkk. A function is (k,ε)(k,\varepsilon)(k,ε)-band-limited if that weight is at most ε\varepsilonε: almost all of its "energy" is at low frequency. For a ±1\pm1±1-valued function the total weight is 111, so the tail is the fraction of energy above degree kkk.

3 How this is formalised

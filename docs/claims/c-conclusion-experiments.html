Section 9, paragraph 3Conclusion: what the saved experiments do and do not show

Informal interpretation Motivation, interpretation or scope; not a formal claim.

1 Paper text

The saved floating-point experiments show a large supervised two-frame restoration advantage and perfect finite-test P/NP/NP/N classification by a learned multi-head controller. The latter coexists with imperfect individual channel recovery. The restoration plateaus and checkpoint diagnostics also show that learning can proceed in stages after transition information is supplied. These observations do not certify the formal gate assumptions, worst-case mastery, or self-play discovery.

Appendix C. Implementation details: shallow circuits and fixed-scale networks

Generated from paper_jmlr.tex, lines 1464–1464.

2 Explain this

Appendix C is the engineering behind the bypasses. All constructions share a few shallow operations: a heap is a BBB-bit block; a move is a validity bit, a binary heap index and a BBB-bit value. The circuits have polynomial size in their supplied input length and depth independent of NNN and BBB. Every XOR called shallow combines two bits (or a fixed number under a bounded-change promise); an XOR of arbitrarily many rows is never treated as a primitive.

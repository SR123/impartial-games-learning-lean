Lemma B.3A layer of polynomially many fixed-scale units is simulated with constant depth and polynomial size

Checked theorem Lean checks a formal statement corresponding to this claim.

1 Paper text

Lemma B.3 (One layer of fixed-scale quantised units is in AC0\AC^0AC0) Fix W,DW,DW,D and let a layer consist of m′m'm′ threshold units whose inputs are outputs of a previous layer of width mmm, with m,m′≤poly(n)m,m'\le \poly(n)m,m′≤poly(n). Then the layer can be simulated by an AC0\AC^0AC0 circuit of constant depth and polynomial size.

Generated from paper_jmlr.tex, lines 1445–1449.

2 Explain this

A layer is m′m'm′ units side by side. Apply the one-unit lemma to each and place the circuits in parallel: depth stays 222 and size is m′⋅O(mK)m'\cdot O(m^K)m′⋅O(mK), polynomial. Composing a constant number of layers then proves Theorem 2.4.

3 How this is formalised

  • AC0.eval_compileLayer: substituting the previous layer's formulas into each gate's DNF computes the layer (AC0.compileLayer).
  • AC0.compileLayer_size: each output's size is at most G⋅(S+1)G\cdot(S+1)G⋅(S+1) with G=1+(M+1)W(2W+1)G=1+(M+1)^W(2W+1)G=1+(M+1)W(2W+1), for previous formulas of size at most SSS and fan-in at most MMM.
  • AC0.compileLayer_depth: depth grows by at most 333 per layer.

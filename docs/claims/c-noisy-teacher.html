2 Explain this

The noisy teacher plays the optimal move (to nim-sum zero) with probability 1−ε1-\varepsilon1−ε and otherwise a uniformly random legal move. Random moves broaden the positions seen and, for two-frame agents, create varied transition pairs from which the difference signal can be learned.

Note: the dataset actually reported in Section 7.2 is generated by a specific teacher–random–teacher scheme (optimal move, then one uniformly random move, then the optimal labels), not by sampling games from πTε\pi_T^\varepsilonπTε​ with a stated ε\varepsilonε.

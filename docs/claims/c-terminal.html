1 Paper text

A position with no legal moves is terminal.

Generated from paper_jmlr.tex, lines 404–404.

2 Explain this

A terminal position is one with no legal moves. In NIM, these are exactly the boards whose heaps are all empty; the player to move there has lost (normal play).

3 How this is formalised

Game.Terminal defines a terminal board as one whose heaps are all zero, and Game.terminal_iff_no_move proves that this is equivalent to having no legal move.

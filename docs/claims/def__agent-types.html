Definition 5.7The restricted measurement interface: root and one-heap queries to one fixed structured head, bounded readout

1 Paper text

Definition 5.7 (Restricted rollout measurements and readout) Fix a root position PPP. An admissible query starts at PPP or a position obtained by changing one heap of PPP; the changed index and old/new values are known to the readout. A legal one-move successor is an admissible query. Arbitrary transformations of the whole root are excluded. For the single-channel lower bound, one fixes a policy πj\pi_jπj​ from Proposition 5.9, independently of PPP. Writing β=ROπj(P)\beta=\mathrm{RO}_{\pi_j}(P)β=ROπj​​(P), the agent’s final move must have the form FN(P,β)F_N(P,\beta)FN​(P,β), where FNF_NFN​ is a polynomial-size, constant-depth Boolean circuit family. Any value head, query corrections or other processing must be included in this readout bound. The number of queries alone does not impose that bound. A multi-channel construction may query distinct members of the same structured policy family and apply a polynomial-size, constant-depth readout to PPP and their outcome bits. A policy head means a rollout policy, not simply a parallel output neuron. We count simulated game moves separately from the circuit work per move.

2 Explain this

This definition fixes exactly what the single-channel lower bound allows.

  • Queries. Start a rollout at the root board PPP, or at a board obtained by changing one heap of PPP (for example a candidate successor). Rollouts from arbitrary transformed boards are not allowed.
  • One channel. The policy is one fixed member πj\pi_jπj​ of the structured family, chosen independently of PPP.
  • Bounded readout. The final move must be computed by a polynomial-size, constant-depth circuit from PPP and the observed bit β\betaβ. Any value head, correction of queries or other processing counts towards that readout.

The multi-channel construction may query several members of the family and then apply such a readout. A "policy head" means a rollout policy, not a parallel output neuron.

3 How this is formalised

The interface appears in Lean through its consequences rather than as a single definition.

  • One-heap queries: Prob.single_policy_affine and Misc.single_policy_correction show that a rollout from a board with one heap changed from hhh to h′h'h′ returns the root outcome XOR a correction bit computable from h⊕h′h\oplus h'h⊕h′, so such queries add no new unknown bit.
  • Why arbitrary starts are excluded: Misc.bitBoard_query (next record).
  • The readout bound: BinReadout.BBound specifies binary readout formulas of depth at most ddd and size at most sss; the conditional theorem quantifies over all such readouts. The correlation premise for their indicators is Prob.CorrHyp.

Prose-to-formal notes

In the conditional bound the readout is any family of bounded-depth, polynomial-size binary circuits receiving the board and the observed bit. Query corrections must be computed inside that readout.

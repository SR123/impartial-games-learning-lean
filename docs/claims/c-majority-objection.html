2 Explain this

A natural objection: real networks compute majority-like functions, so maybe TC0\mathrm{TC}^0TC0 (threshold circuits) is the right model, not AC0. Indeed, with scale-adaptive thresholds or fan-in-dependent rescaling, constant-depth threshold models can compute parity.

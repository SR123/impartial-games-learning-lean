2 Explain this

The validation curve, one point per epoch: 1F stays near 0.08; 2F rises quickly to a plateau near 0.54, stays there from about epoch 8 to about epoch 30 in the plot, then climbs steeply to near-perfect accuracy.

The plateau suggests a learning bottleneck that training eventually overcomes. It is a different thing from the representational lower bound: the 2F model can represent the solution, and eventually finds it.

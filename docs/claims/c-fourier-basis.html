2 Explain this

Characters. For a set SSS of input coordinates, χS(x)=(−1)∑i∈Sxi\chi_S(x)=(-1)^{\sum_{i\in S}x_i}χS​(x)=(−1)∑i∈S​xi​ is +1+1+1 or −1-1−1 according to the parity of the inputs in SSS.

Basis. The 2n2^n2n characters are orthonormal under the uniform distribution, and every real function fff on {0,1}n\{0,1\}^n{0,1}n is a unique combination f=∑Sf^(S)χSf=\sum_S\hat f(S)\chi_Sf=∑S​f^​(S)χS​ with coefficients f^(S)=E[fχS]\hat f(S)=\mathbb E[f\chi_S]f^​(S)=E[fχS​].

Example. On two bits, AND (as a 0/1 function) is $\tfrac14-\tfrac14\chi_{{1}}-\tfrac14\chi_{{2}}+\tfrac14 \chi_{{1,2}}$.

The size ∣S∣|S|∣S∣ is the degree or frequency of the coefficient. Parity of all inputs is the single highest-degree character.

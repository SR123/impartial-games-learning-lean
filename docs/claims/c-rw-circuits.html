1 Paper text

Circuit complexity and neural networks. Parity is outside AC0\AC^0AC0 (Furst et al., 1984; Håstad, 1986), while constant-depth threshold circuits (TC0\TC^0TC0) can compute it. Attention results depend on the precise model: Hao, Angluin and Frank analyse hard-attention variants, including AC0\AC^0AC0 restrictions for unique hard attention (Hao et al., 2022). Merrill and Sabharwal study the additional power of intermediate decoding over a TC0\TC^0TC0 baseline (Merrill and Sabharwal, 2024); that containment does not rule out parity. Their sequential-computation perspective is relevant to our recurrent bypass, although the architectures and formal assumptions differ. Our contribution applies these distinctions to NIM policies and a restricted rollout-measurement interface.

2 Explain this

Related work on circuits and neural models: parity is outside AC0 but inside TC0\mathrm{TC}^0TC0; results on attention depend on the exact model (hard attention can be in AC0; other variants have TC0\mathrm{TC}^0TC0 bounds that do not rule out parity).

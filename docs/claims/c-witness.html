Section 2.1, “Why fixed-scale quantisation suggests an AC0 abstraction”Fixed scale gives a witness of at most W literals, hence a constant-width DNF for each unit

1 Paper text

Why fixed-scale quantisation suggests an AC0\AC^0AC0 abstraction. The key structural property is fixed scale: the smallest nonzero weight is 1/D1/D1/D and the largest threshold is W/DW/DW/D, with W,DW,DW,D independent of the input length. Consequently, whenever a threshold unit outputs 111, there is a satisfying witness consisting of at most WWW input literals whose contributions already cross the threshold. This yields a constant-width DNF expansion for each unit (Lemma B.1), and composing a constant number of layers yields an AC0\AC^0AC0 simulation (Theorem 2.4).

2 Explain this

The idea. With fixed scale, the smallest positive weight is 1/D1/D1/D and the largest threshold is W/DW/DW/D. If a unit fires, its weighted sum reached the threshold; each active literal contributes at least 1/D1/D1/D, so at most WWW of them already suffice. Such a small set of literals is a witness.

Consequence. A unit fires exactly when some witness of at most WWW literals is fully true: an OR over at most (m≤W)\binom{m}{\le W}(≤Wm​) ANDs of at most WWW literals. That is a DNF of polynomial size O(mW)O(m^W)O(mW) and depth 2, an AC0 circuit. Composing a constant number of layers keeps everything in AC0.

Caveat. If thresholds could grow with fan-in (for example majority, threshold m/2m/2m/2), witnesses would grow too, and this argument would fail.

3 How this is formalised

AC0.Gate.bounded_witness: whenever a gate fires, an initial segment of its active literals of length at most the integer threshold b≤Wb\le Wb≤W already has coefficient sum at least bbb. AC0.gate_ac0 turns this into the DNF, with exact semantics and the explicit size and depth bounds. See Lemma B.1 for the full lemma.

2 Explain this

Statement. A single unit f(z)=H(∑jwjzj−θ)f(z)=\mathbf H(\sum_j w_jz_j-\theta)f(z)=H(∑j​wj​zj​−θ) with grid weights and threshold is computed by a depth-2 AC0 circuit (an OR of ANDs) of size O(mW)O(m^W)O(mW).

Proof idea. Multiply by DDD: f(z)=1f(z)=1f(z)=1 iff ∑jajzj≥b\sum_j a_jz_j\ge b∑j​aj​zj​≥b with integers 0≤aj,b≤W0\le a_j,b\le W0≤aj​,b≤W. If b=0b=0b=0 the unit is constantly 111. Otherwise each active input contributes at least 111, so if the sum reaches bbb, greedily choosing active inputs reaches bbb after at most b≤Wb\le Wb≤W steps. Hence f(z)=1f(z)=1f(z)=1 iff some set TTT of at most WWW inputs with ∑j∈Taj≥b\sum_{j\in T}a_j\ge b∑j∈T​aj​≥b is all true: an OR over at most ∑r≤W(mr)=O(mW)\sum_{r\le W}\binom mr=O(m^W)∑r≤W​(rm​)=O(mW) ANDs.

Example. W=2W=2W=2, D=1D=1D=1, weights (1,1,2)(1,1,2)(1,1,2), threshold 222: f=(z1∧z2)∨z3f=(z_1\wedge z_2)\vee z_3f=(z1​∧z2​)∨z3​.

3 How this is formalised

  • AC0.Gate.bounded_witness: if the gate fires, an initial segment of its active literals, of length at most the threshold, already reaches the threshold.
  • AC0.gate_ac0: the DNF AC0.dnf computes the gate exactly; depth at most 333 when NOT nodes are counted; size at most 1+(m+1)W(2W+1)1+(m+1)^W(2W+1)1+(m+1)W(2W+1); it reads only the gate's inputs. Edge cases b=0b=0b=0, m=0m=0m=0 and W=0W=0W=0 are included.
  • ClassicalBoundary.dnf_aoDepth: in the classical convention (negations on inputs not counted) the same DNF has AND/OR depth at most 222, as the lemma states.

Prose-to-formal notes

Lean's depth counts NOT gates and gives depth at most 3; in the classical convention (negations on inputs not counted) the same DNF has AND/OR depth 2. The Lean size bound is 1+(m+1)W(2W+1)1+(m+1)^W(2W+1)1+(m+1)W(2W+1).

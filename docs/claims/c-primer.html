1 Paper text

Circuit complexity primer. AC0\AC^0AC0 is the class of Boolean functions computable by constant-depth, polynomial-size circuits with unbounded fan-in AND/OR/NOT gates. A classical result is that PARITY∉AC0\mathrm{PARITY}\notin\AC^0PARITY∈/AC0. In contrast, TC0\TC^0TC0 augments AC0\AC^0AC0 with unbounded fan-in threshold gates and can compute parity. Our focus here is a fixed-scale quantised regime in which thresholds do not scale with fan-in; in that regime, each threshold unit admits a constant-witness DNF expansion and the overall computation collapses to AC0\AC^0AC0 (Theorem 2.4).

2 Explain this

Background in one paragraph: AC0 is constant depth, polynomial size, unbounded fan-in AND, OR and NOT; PARITY is not in AC0 (a classical theorem); TC0 adds threshold gates of unbounded fan-in and can compute parity. In the fixed-scale regime each threshold unit has a constant-size witness, so the computation collapses to AC0.

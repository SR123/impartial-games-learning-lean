2 Explain this

The finite form of the single-channel bound: if every readout indicator has correlation at most ηN\eta_NηN​ with every nonconstant character of the nim-sum (under the unconditioned uniform distribution), then a legal winning move is found with probability at most 2/(q−1)+(q−1)ηN2/(q-1)+(q-1)\eta_N2/(q−1)+(q−1)ηN​ given σ≠0\sigma\ne0σ=0, where q=2Bq=2^Bq=2B. The premise is a two-sided correlation bound; it does not assume independence between readout and nim-sum.

3 How this is formalised

Prob.success_bound, for every B≥1B\ge1B≥1, N≥1N\ge1N≥1, every readout and every η\etaη, with the premise Prob.CorrHyp as a hypothesis of the theorem (not an axiom). The inequality is proved outright from its stated premise; the premise is supplied for AC0 readouts only through the classical input (see Theorem 5.12 (i)).

Proof coverage

Scope: what is and is not covered

This page records, claim by claim, how the paper's statements are supported. It is a map of evidence, not a certificate for the paper as a whole.

  • What Lean checks. The Lean development states formal versions of the paper's definitions, theorems and constructions, and the Lean kernel checks every proof. A successful build establishes the formal statement. Whether that statement says the same thing as an English sentence is a separate question; each claim record answers it in plain language under How this is formalised and names any gap.
  • Named classical inputs. The classical circuit lower bounds are not proved here. Håstad's negligible parity-correlation theorem enters as an explicit hypothesis, HastadNegl, of the theorems that need it; the worst-case statement ParityNotAC0 is derived from it. Results that use it are marked checked conditional theorem using named classical input. The Linial–Mansour–Nisan and Tal spectral bounds are cited only: the Fourier-tail hypothesis TailPremise is used in a checked implication, but it has not been connected to a precise published statement with reconciled conventions.
  • What the counts mean. The counts further down include helper lemmas, adapters and auxiliary definitions; they measure the size of the development, not the number of results in the paper.
  • What is not certified. Training runs, learning curves, checkpoint diagnostics and the cause of the plateau near 0.54 are empirical. They rest on saved run records and are marked empirical result. The experiments do not show that AlphaZero-style self-play discovers any of the constructions. Background literature and interpretive remarks are marked external cited result or informal interpretation.
  • Bridges that are absent. The recurrent replay and board-storage strategies are proved to win in their own stateful game semantics. A bridge from that semantics to the newer general history model of weak mastery (Mastery.Hist) has not been built; the audit lists it as optional. Until it exists, those guarantees hold in the controller's own semantics only. The website reports this rather than hiding it.
  • The full sequential bypasses are not AC0. Each bypass keeps individual updates shallow while adding information or processing time. The complete multi-head and recurrent board-to-move maps are proved not to be AC0 families (conditional on ParityNotAC0); nothing here says that full parity computation is in AC0.

Lean's axiom basis

Every theorem in the development was checked by the Lean kernel in the fresh rebuild, and the build printed the axioms each theorem depends on. Only Lean's three standard axioms occur (propositional extensionality, the axiom of choice and quotient soundness). No sorry, custom axiom or unchecked code was found by the audit. These axioms are the ordinary foundation of Lean and Mathlib; they are different from the named mathematical hypotheses described above, which appear as explicit premises of the conditional theorems.
